About Linnea

Linnea is a health log for you and your family on iPhone and Apple Watch. Log meals, water, medications and vitals in seconds by voice, photo, Siri or Apple Watch. Linnea reads sleep, activity and heart data from Apple Health, combines it with what you log into one daily score, and saves meals, water, workouts, sleep, mood, symptoms and vitals back to Apple Health. Paper lab reports are scanned on the iPhone into health records, which are encrypted on the device before upload. A family of up to six people can share daily summaries by choice and keep the log and records for children, or for parents without a phone.

Linnea is made by Arctura Technologies Inc. in Ottawa, Canada. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
