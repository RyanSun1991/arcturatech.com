How we calculate dish calories and nutrition

Standard dishes

Each dish is a standard recipe written by the Linnea team: how many grams of each ingredient are in 100 g of the finished dish. Only the edible part counts, and oil, salt and sugar follow common amounts. Every dish page lists these ingredients.

Ingredient by ingredient

Each ingredient is matched to one row of an official food composition table, and the rows are added up by grams to give the energy and nutrients per 100 g. A nutrient is shown only when the ingredients that carry it make up at least 90% of the dish's weight. The table behind each ingredient is named next to it.

Releases

The values on these pages are calculated from release 2026-10-18 of Linnea's standard dishes, the same data as the standard dishes in the Linnea app. The edition of each table is listed below.

What an estimate means

A standard dish is one common way to cook it, not the plate on your table. Different ingredients, oil and portions give different numbers. In Linnea you can save a dish as your family's recipe and calculate it from your own ingredients.
