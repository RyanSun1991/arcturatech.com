Linnea Consumer Health Data Policy

Linnea: Health Companion · Version 2.0 · Effective October 20, 2026

This policy supplements the Linnea Privacy Policy. It describes how we handle consumer health data, as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada’s consumer health data law and similar laws, and the rights those laws give you. Linnea is provided by its developer, as named on Linnea’s App Store and Google Play listings (“we,” “us” or “our”).

1. Consumer health data we collect

  • Profile details such as date of birth, sex, height, weight, dietary preferences, allergies and goals.
  • What you log: food, drinks and water; medications and supplements, taken or skipped; exercise and sleep; mood and symptoms; body measurements, blood pressure, pulse, blood glucose and body temperature; and notes. Data entered with earlier versions of Linnea, such as medication doses or menstrual cycle phase, stays in your account.
  • Photos and voice recordings you submit for logging, and the food searches and barcode numbers you send to look up foods, which aren’t stored with your account.
  • Daily totals, workouts and nights of sleep from Apple Health or Health Connect, with your permission. Raw samples stay on your device.
  • Health records, and information logged for managed family members, which are encrypted on your device; we can’t read them.
  • What family members share with you, care alerts, meals shared in your family and family recipes.
  • Scores, trends and the results of AI features you use.

2. Sources

We get this data from you; from Apple Health or Health Connect, with your permission; from calculations on your device and our servers; from family members who choose to share with you; and from service providers that return a result you ask for, such as an AI result or a transcription.

3. How we use it

We use consumer health data to provide the features you ask for, such as logging, scores, trends, reminders, plans, health records, family sharing, export and saving to Apple Health or Health Connect; to provide AI features you turn on; to keep Linnea secure and working; to respond to your requests; and to comply with the law. We don’t sell consumer health data or use it for advertising, and we don’t use it for decisions about credit, employment, insurance, housing or eligibility for healthcare.

4. Sharing

We share consumer health data only:

  • with service providers that process it for us: our hosting provider (all of the categories above, with health records and managed members’ information encrypted so it can’t read them), our AI providers (the photo, text, recording, entries and daily totals an AI feature you turn on needs) and our crash reporting and analytics providers (no health values);
  • with Apple or Google, when you choose to save entries to Apple Health or Health Connect;
  • with the family members you choose, as you direct; and
  • where the law requires it, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

Health records and information logged for managed members are never sent to an AI provider.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm whether we collect or share your consumer health data, to give you access to it with a list of the third parties and affiliates we have shared it with, to delete it, or to withdraw your consent. Deletion extends to our service providers and backups as the law requires. You can also export your data, delete entries and delete your account directly in the app.

To make a request, email privacy@arcturatech.com with “Consumer Health Data Request” in the subject. We may ask for information to verify that the account is yours. If we decline your request, we’ll tell you why and how to appeal: reply with “Privacy Appeal” in the subject. If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Attorney General of your state, such as the Washington State Attorney General. We won’t discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

6. Consent and changes

Access to Apple Health and Health Connect is optional and controlled by your device’s permissions, and saving to them can be turned off in the app. AI features run only after you agree, and family sharing and care alerts are optional and can be stopped at any time. Before using consumer health data in a materially different way, we’ll ask for any consent the law requires. Updates to this policy will have a new effective date.
