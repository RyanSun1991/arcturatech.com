PRIVACY POLICY

Armilla Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · Effective {EFFECTIVE_DATE}

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled across Armilla: the Armilla app for iPhone (which can also be installed on iPad as an iPhone app), including its App Clip for opening invitations without installing the app; the Armilla app for Android phones; the web app at armilla.arcturatech.com; the product, legal, and invitation pages at arcturatech.com/armilla; and the services behind them (hosted on Supabase in Singapore). One Armilla account and one membership work across iPhone, Android, and the web. You can open this page at any time in the app under Me → About → Terms & privacy. This policy is provided in English only. If you read version 1.2, Section 14 lists everything new in version 1.3.

The short version: You can use Armilla without an account, or turn on Local-only mode so nothing leaves your device. When you sign in, birth details are stored with field-level encryption (AES-256-GCM), while journal text, life-event notes, life-event photos, Ask memories, and Pro client notes are end-to-end encrypted on your devices — we cannot read or see them. AI features are available only to signed-in adults (18+) and never run on a minor's profile; OpenAI and Google (paid Gemini API) are our named AI providers (see Section 8 for which one each feature uses right now), and neither uses your API content to train its models. Palm and face photo uploads require a separate, explicit consent. We never sell or rent your personal information, show no ads, and use no third-party analytics or tracking tools. You can export your data or delete single items or your entire account at any time in Me → Privacy & data.

You can reach us regarding privacy at privacy@arcturatech.com. For product help, reach us at support@arcturatech.com.

2. Using Armilla without an account, and Local-only mode

Using Armilla without an account

When you use Armilla without signing in, your charts, journal entries, life events (including their notes and photos), divination records, and assessment records stay on your device (or, in the web app, in that browser's storage) and nowhere else.

Even without an account, four things can still involve a network request:

  • Usage data: Anonymous usage statistics, when enabled under the rules in Section 3.
  • Requests you start yourself: For example, opening an invitation link someone sent you.
  • Free chart web addresses on the web app: Every free chart on armilla.arcturatech.com has its own web address, which is also its share link. That address carries the name you typed, the birth date, the birthplace, the sex used for the calculation if you chose one, and any non-default chart settings — never the birth time, which stays only in that browser's storage. When the page is opened, the server that serves the web app (Vercel) receives that web address.
  • Purchases made without signing in: If you make a purchase in the app without signing in, the app creates an anonymous account on our server solely to hold that purchase and its membership (with no e-mail address). When you later sign in, the purchase moves to your signed-in account and the anonymous account is deleted.

Local-only mode

You can turn on Local-only mode at Me → Privacy & data → Local mode. When it is on, the app sends no business requests at all: cloud sync, AI features, invitations, the Family plan, purchases, and usage statistics all stop, while everything stored on your device continues to work locally.

If you are signed in when you turn on Local-only mode, you choose between two options: "pause sync and sign out (your cloud data is kept)" or "delete cloud data" (which deletes your cloud data in the same way as deleting your account under Section 11). Either way, any profiles that other people shared with you are removed from that device, because a withdrawal by them can only reach your device through sync.

3. What information we collect

Everything collected when you sign in or use online features is linked to your account. None of it is used for advertising or combined with data from other companies.

  • Account information: Your e-mail address and an internal account number. You can sign in with Apple (which may provide a relay e-mail address), with Google, or with a six-digit code we e-mail to you. The sign-in code e-mail is sent from our company mailbox through Google's Gmail (Section 8), which handles your e-mail address and the code in order to deliver it. We never receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Birth details: Birth date, birth time, birthplace, how sure you are of the time, and the sex used for the calculation — for you and for people you add. On our server they are stored with field-level encryption (AES-256-GCM), with the encryption keys kept separate from the database. Only a small number of server functions decrypt them (chart calculation, listing your profiles, generating your data export, the age check, and birth-time inference), and every decryption is written to an audit record. For your own account, the server keeps only an age band (under 13, 13–17, or adult), never a copy of your birth date.
  • Names and profile pictures: Display names for you and for the people you add. If you choose a profile picture, it is re-encoded on your device to at most 512 pixels without metadata and stored in private storage that only your account can read.
  • End-to-end encrypted content: Journal text; life-event descriptions and reflections; divination questions and outcome notes; the memories Ask Armilla keeps (What Armilla remembers); and Pro client notes and tags. These are encrypted on your device before upload (XChaCha20-Poly1305), and the key is never sent to our server — we cannot read them. We see only technical details such as dates and counts.
  • Life-event photos (end-to-end encrypted): Up to 4 photos per life event. Once you are signed in and end-to-end encryption is set up, life-event photos sync between your devices on the iPhone app, the Android app, and the web app. Each photo is re-encoded on your device (location and device metadata removed, and the original file name is not kept), encrypted with a key of its own that only your devices can open, and uploaded to your private cloud storage — we cannot see the photos, and no AI ever receives them. We can see only which life event a photo belongs to, how many photos each event has, their order, the encrypted file's approximate size (in 64 KB steps), and the upload time. While you are signed out, in Local-only mode, or before end-to-end encryption is set up, life-event photos stay only on that device.
  • Metadata needed for calculations (synced in plain form): Life-event date, precision, kind, area of life, and mood; journal date, mood, and pattern tags; the divination hexagram; and your accuracy ratings of events. The Life Map and pattern calculations require these fields.
  • Assessment scores: Results of full questionnaires (16 types, nine types, Big Five), their version and date, and your history of retakes. Your answers to individual questions, drafts, and the 12-question quick estimate stay on your device.
  • Ratings you give of other people ("how you see them"): Scores and types only (Section 5).
  • AI conversations and readings: Your Ask Armilla questions and answers, saved readings and analyses, palm and face photos and readings after your separate consent, and birth-time inference records (Section 6).
  • Purchases and entitlements: Store transaction numbers, product, dates, and status (from Apple App Store, Google Play, or on the web through Stripe). We never see your payment card details. Free membership periods (for example from an invitation) are stored as entitlement records, not purchases. For web payments disputed with your bank, we keep a dispute record (Section 10). You can review your plan and purchases at Me → Membership & purchases (Plan, Family members, My purchases).
  • Devices and notifications: The push token registered for notifications (Apple APNs on iPhone / Google FCM on Android) and a record of the notifications we queued or sent (type and date only, with no message text), kept for 90 days.
  • Preferences that follow you across devices: Chart settings, display density, your daily reminder settings, accuracy weights, the home screen you chose, and up to the three profiles you looked at most recently.
  • Usage data: Our own usage events — only fixed codes, counts, yes/no values, and durations, never anything you typed and never birth details. Usage data is off by default in the EU / EEA, the UK, and Switzerland until you agree; on by default elsewhere (the region comes from your device or browser language settings); off by default for accounts aged 13–17, who can turn it on; and never collected for under-13s or in Local-only mode. Switch: Me → Privacy & data → Usage data. We use no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising tools.
  • Security records: To prevent abuse, public endpoints (such as the invitation page) store a one-way cryptographic hash of the visitor's IP address, using a salt that changes daily, for at most 8 days; it cannot be turned back into the IP address.
  • E-mails you send us: Your e-mail address and what you write to us.

What we do not access: We do not access your contacts, your device's location, health data, or advertising identifiers. You type the birthplace yourself, and the place search runs offline on your device.

4. What stays on your device unless you choose otherwise

Each of the following stays on your device under the condition stated:

  • Palm & face without the AI reading consent: The camera image is analysed in memory on your device (Apple Vision on iPhone; Google ML Kit and MediaPipe models running on the device on Android). Only coarse feature classes and their confidence are kept, in local storage that never syncs and is not backed up, and only for your own profile. Keeping the original photo is off by default; if turned on, it also stays on the device and out of backups. When you ask for a reading on your phone, only the conclusions the phone drew (for example, "the hand shape leans to water") — never the photo — go to the AI provider (Section 6). With your separate consent, photos can be uploaded and read by the AI (Section 6).
  • Voice dictation and reading aloud: Dictating a journal entry or an Ask Armilla question uses only your device's offline speech recognition; the audio is not saved to a file and not sent anywhere. Dictation is offered only where your device has an offline recogniser for the language, and the resulting text becomes an ordinary journal entry or question. Reading an answer aloud uses your device's system voice.
  • Life-event photos while not syncing: As noted in Section 3, life-event photos stay only on your device when you are signed out, in Local-only mode, or before end-to-end encryption is set up.
  • Share images (essence card, six-rings card): Generated on your device and sent only where you choose through the system share sheet. They contain no birth date, birth time, or birthplace, and no score, and are never made for a minor's profile.
  • Individual questionnaire answers and place search: Answers to each assessment question, unfinished drafts, the 12-question quick estimate, and the offline birthplace database stay on your device.
  • System permission prompts: Prompts for the camera, microphone, speech recognition, and saving to your photo library are used only for these features.

5. Other people's details, invitations, and sharing

Details you fill in for others

When you save birth details for another person, you confirm that you have their agreement before saving; for a minor, you confirm as their parent or legal guardian. A minor's profile is never used by any AI feature.

Inviting someone to fill in their own details

You can send someone a one-time invitation link, valid for 14 days by default. They need no account: they fill in their own birth details on the invitation page, in the iPhone App Clip, or in the app, confirm they are 18 or older, and agree before submitting. They choose how much to share with you:

  • Full: You see their birth date and birth time.
  • Results only: You see the chart results, not their birth date, birth time, or birthplace.
  • Just this one compatibility reading: Shared only for that single reading.

When finished, they receive a read-only link to see the compatibility reading from their side; we keep only a hash of that link's token, and it stops working after 30 days. The inviter's birth details never appear in their result. They can also tick "keep these details for me too" (unticked by default): we then keep a separately encrypted copy only for them, which is deleted at 30 days, when they sign up and link it to an account, or when they withdraw — whichever comes first.

When an invited person signs up and links the result, both of you see the shared reading under People; signing up through an invitation gives each of you 7 days of Plus (an entitlement record only).

Withdrawal of sharing

Either side can stop sharing at any time (the invited person can also withdraw through their original link). When sharing ends, the shared reading is deleted at once, AI answers that used their details are replaced by a short neutral note, related reports are emptied, and their birth details and everything calculated from them are deleted within 24 hours (usually within 15 minutes). What you wrote yourself (for example, your own event notes or Pro client notes) is kept.

Family space (Family plan)

A Family plan supports up to 6 members (managed under Me → Membership & purchases → Family members). Joining shares nothing by default. Each member decides whether to appear in the Family space and whether to share their birthday (month and day only, never the year), take part in the family climate (day master), cycle milestones (to the year only), and up to 12 insight cards (summaries without the reasoning). Turning any option off deletes it from the Family space. Members aged 13–17 can share only some of these items, and the Family space is not available to under-13s. If the Family plan owner deletes their account, the family ends, and members keep all their own data.

Answering for someone else ("how you see them")

Questionnaires you fill in about another person ("how you see them") are your private record: that person never sees them (even if linked to you), they are never treated as that person's own result or used in any chart calculation, and they reach the AI only as your view when you ask about that person. They are exported and deleted with your account, and deleted when you delete that profile.

Pro clients

If you keep client profiles with Pro, you are responsible for obtaining your clients' consent. Client notes and tags are end-to-end encrypted on your device. A practitioner's note for a session report is kept encrypted on the server only while the report is being made, and is not included in your data export.

6. AI features

How Armilla uses AI providers

Two companies can process the content of Armilla's AI features: OpenAI (through the OpenAI API) and Google (through the paid Gemini API). Each AI feature is handled by one of them at a time, chosen by us per feature, and we can move a feature from one to the other. Whichever company handles a feature receives what this section lists for that feature: for text features, the chart facts, your question or conversation, and the notes or summaries you allowed; for AI palm & face reading, the photos. Both companies are named here so that moving a feature between them does not require a new agreement from you. Section 8 has a table showing which company each AI feature uses now; when you open this page, the table checks Armilla's server for the current assignment, so a switch on our server shows up without this page being edited or republished (with a fallback to the published assignment if needed). The palm & face consent page also names the company that reads the photos.

Both companies act on our behalf under their API terms and do not use this content to train or improve their models: OpenAI's API data terms state that data sent to the API is not used to train or improve its models, and Google's terms for the paid Gemini API state that it does not use prompts (including images) or responses to improve its products. To detect abuse, they may keep requests for a short period: OpenAI for up to 30 days, and Google states "a limited period" without specifying a number of days.

AI notice at sign-in: AI features require an account. The sign-in screen shows a short, one-time notice stating that AI features send content to these companies for processing; signing in means you agree to that processing as described in this policy. Accounts aged 13–17 and under-13s receive no AI features at all.

What the AI does not do: Every statement the AI makes about a chart cites an element our chart engine computed. It answers questions about the future with a direction, a leaning, and its grounds. It never predicts death, illness, disaster, the outcome of a lawsuit, which investment to pick, or lottery results, and it does not diagnose.

Ask Armilla (the Ask tab)

Ask Armilla requires an account and is not available to accounts under 18. For each question, the request sent to the company handling Ask's answers contains:

  • Your question and the earlier messages of that conversation;
  • The chart facts our engine computed for the subject (you, someone you added, or a relationship) — never the birth date, birth time, or birthplace themselves. For someone who shared results only, facts that could reveal their birth time are removed. Minors' profiles, people who withdrew, and people outside a "just this reading" share never take part;
  • Your answers about that person ("how you see them"), marked as your view;
  • The memories you saved (What Armilla remembers — on by default, though a memory exists only once you save one): decrypted on your device and sent with the question, while our server keeps them only end-to-end encrypted;
  • When "Let AI read my life events" is on (off by default): summaries made on your device of your life events and recent journal entries (up to 20 events, each within a length limit), sent for that single answer only — we do not store or log them;
  • When you tap "Ask about this" on one of your own palm & face readings (or one of its observations): the reading's text goes with each question while the reading stays shown in the question bar — specifically, the corrected observations with their confidence and your corrections, how they were matched with your chart facts, the reading's kind, date, depth, title, and the opening of each section (about 2,000 characters in all), plus up to two passages citing an observation if you asked about one observation. No photo, and no photo number or link, is ever sent. This applies only to the adult account holder's own readings, only while the current version of the palm & face consent is agreed (not after you withdraw it, and not after the consent's wording changes until you agree again), and only when you ask — you can remove the reading from the question bar at any time, and the server keeps no copy between questions.

Separately, your message on its own (without the chart, conversation history, memories, or any reading) goes to the company handling Ask's safety check and suggestions to decide whether to point you to professional help or whether the question asks for a prediction Armilla never makes, and to a small extra pass that may suggest recording a life event or saving a memory — a suggestion is saved only if you confirm it.

Questions and answers are stored on our server (not end-to-end encrypted); you can delete a conversation at any time. If an answer restates a summary, a memory, or a reading, that text is stored with the conversation. Starred answers are kept as a list of which messages you starred. To notice over-reliance, the server keeps short theme labels of questions (with no message text) for 7 days.

AI readings and analyses

AI readings and analyses require an account, are not available to accounts under 18, and never run on a minor's profile (a minor sees the rule library text instead). This covers readings you ask for in Readings and the Life Map by theme, by system, by length (Brief, Full, Long), or by period (My readings); the star map's synthesis; system pages' readings; Today's guidance; relationship readings; AI palm & face reading; and the text of reports you buy. For each generation, the company handling that feature receives:

  • The chart of the people concerned as our engine computed it — for My readings and Ask Armilla, the complete chart (pillars, palaces and stars, planets and aspects, luck-period and dasha sequences, energy-centre and channel layout, numbers, and so on), plus, for a period reading, the cycle state of that time, which our server works out for that one request from the encrypted birth details and does not keep; together with the rule library's written readings and short excerpts from our library — never the birth date, birth time, birthplace, or names;
  • My readings (the readings asked for by theme, system, length, or period) run only for your own profile or an adult who shared fully with you, and never for people who withdrew or minors' profiles;
  • Other analyses (the star map's synthesis, system readings, relationship readings, today's guidance, and report text) follow Ask Armilla's sharing, withdrawal, and minor protections instead: someone who shared results only can take part—but without the chart facts that could reveal their birth time—while a "just this one compatibility reading" share takes part only inside that one pair, and people who withdrew and minors' profiles never take part;
  • When "Let AI read my life events" or What Armilla remembers is on, the app's Today guidance and synthesis may include the same on-device summaries and memories for that one generation (the web app does not send these).

The written reading text is stored on our server (not end-to-end encrypted) so that you see the same reading across every device, and is regenerated when your chart data changes. My readings keeps the title, whether it is pinned, and your accurate / partly / not accurate feedback. Stored readings are included in your export. They are deleted with the person, relationship, or account; when someone withdraws, changes their sharing, or is marked as a minor, readings that used their data are deleted; and turning off either switch above deletes readings that used your own words. Report text goes into the report file and is deleted with it. A period reading explains, area by area, whether a time is with you or against you and why.

AI palm & face reading (separate consent)

AI palm & face reading is available when the app offers it, only to the adult account holder, and only for their own hands and face — never for anyone else and never for accounts under 18.

  • Separate consent: It requires a separate consent the first time (apart from the sign-in notice). The consent page states five things: what is uploaded, who analyses it, where it is stored, what is never done, and what is sent when you ask Ask Armilla about a reading. We record when you agreed or withdrew and the version of the text you saw (with a fingerprint of it); when the wording changes, you are asked again before the next upload or analysis.
  • Upload: Only photos you take or choose of your own hands or face. Both the mobile apps and the web app remove location and device details before upload; a file that still carries location details is refused and deleted at once. Photos are kept in your private cloud storage at Armilla (Supabase storage, readable only by your account; download links expire within minutes).
  • Analysis: When you ask for an analysis or a new version, the photos, the observations the model drew from them (for example, "the head line slopes into the mount of the Moon", with a confidence), your corrections, and your own chart facts (for matching with your chart — no birth date, birth time, or birthplace) go to the company handling palm & face photo reading; it looks at the photos, notes what it sees, and writes the reading. If the Brief reading is handled by the other AI company, that company receives only the written observations, your corrections, and your chart facts — never a photo. The providers' API terms and short abuse-monitoring retention apply as described above. Our logs never contain images, photo links, or reading text.
  • Ask about a reading: As described under Ask Armilla above — only the reading's text (never a photo or photo link), and only when you ask.
  • What is stored: The photos, the observations (with confidence), your corrections, and the written readings are kept on our server so you can see them on other devices, compare them by date, and let the observations you choose count in the chart engine's resonance (you can leave any reading out).
  • What is never done: No judgement of health or of how long you will live; no prediction of illness, accidents, or other events; and no face template or feature vector that could identify you, no identification, and no matching with any other data.
  • Delete and withdraw: You can delete any photo or reading at any time (deleting a reading also deletes any photos used only by it), and the stored files are deleted with them. When you withdraw consent, you can choose to delete all photos and readings at once; deleting your account deletes everything; and if an account becomes a minor's, everything is deleted. Without this separate consent, Palm & face still gets the basic on-device analysis described in Section 4.

Because face photos are sensitive or biometric information in some jurisdictions, we process them only with your separate, explicit consent and only to produce the reading you asked for, and send a reading's text to Ask Armilla only when you ask about it.

Birth-time inference (出生时间推定)

Birth-time inference (出生时间推定) is available on the iPhone app, the Android app, and the web app. If you are unsure of a birth time, it ranks likely times from clues you give. It requires an account and an adult account holder, and can be run for you, for an adult whose profile you filled in, or (with Pro) for an adult client whose consent you have — never for a minor's profile, never for a profile someone else filled in and shared with you, and never for a results-only share.

  • AI-assisted inference: Our server keeps the session and works out the result with our chart engine from: a candidate time window and what family members said, the kinds and dates of your recorded life events (at least 5 that qualify), your answers to its questions, and palm & face observations if you chose your own reading as a clue. To write its questions, understand a short free-text answer, and explain the result, it sends the company handling birth-time inference: the differences between candidate times as engine codes; the qualifying events as kind, year and month, precision, areas of life, and mood (never their notes, and never events of bereavement, illness, or "other"); your answers; the palm & face observation labels you chose; how much each clue contributed; and the traditional sayings involved. With "Let AI read my life events" on, the summaries made on your device are also sent (used for that request only and not stored, though the written reasons may quote them). A free-text answer is sent once to be understood and is not stored. Never sent: the birth date, any clock time, the birthplace, the time zone, names, or photos.
  • The encrypted record: Stored encrypted on our server with a server key (AES-256-GCM): the original birth details and time window, the summary of the scan, the events' kinds and dates, your answers and the AI's judgments, the engine's result, and the AI's questions and reasons. Your data export includes it decrypted. When you delete an inference, its content is erased; we keep only the registration (date, the plan or purchase used, and counts) as the record of what was used. Deleting the profile or your account deletes it entirely. An inference that was never finished and had no activity for 30 days is deleted automatically.
  • On this device only: You can instead run birth-time inference on this device only; then nothing except a registration (that you used it once) reaches us.

The result is a likelihood from the systems' traditional rules and a statistical model, not a certain birth time.

7. How we use information and on what basis

  • To provide Armilla (performing our agreement with you): Chart calculation, sync and backup across devices, invitations and sharing, AI features, membership and purchases, and support.
  • Security, abuse prevention, and legal duties (legitimate interests / legal obligations): Keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, maintaining decryption audit records, and meeting tax, payment-dispute, and other legal obligations.
  • Consent (withdrawable at any time): Usage data (where consent applies), "Let AI read my life events", What Armilla remembers, sharing through invitations and the Family space, and — with your separate, explicit consent — AI palm & face reading.
  • Research data (off by default): Only if you turn on Research data (Me → Privacy & data → Research data), de-identified "chart – life event" statistics may be used to improve the systems' accuracy — never your name, event descriptions, or journal text. Turning it off keeps you out of later datasets. No study uses this data yet.

In Canada, we process personal information under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) on the basis of meaningful consent. We do not sell or rent personal information, show no ads, and do not link your data with other companies' data to track you.

9. Security and encryption

  • Transport security: All traffic uses HTTPS (TLS); the mobile apps allow no unencrypted traffic.
  • Server-side field encryption (AES-256-GCM): Birth details, an invited person's separately kept copy, and birth-time inference records are encrypted on the server using AES-256-GCM, with keys kept apart from the database, and are decrypted only in a few audited functions.
  • End-to-end encryption (XChaCha20-Poly1305): Journal text, life-event notes, life-event photos (each photo with its own key, which is itself encrypted), Ask memories, and Pro client notes and tags are encrypted on your devices using XChaCha20-Poly1305. Keys stay only on your devices and in the recovery method you set up (Me → Account & sync → Devices & encryption; Recovery phrase). If every device is lost and there is no recovery phrase, this content cannot be recovered — by us either.
  • Database access control and audit logs: Row-level access control applies to every database table, and audit records can only be appended.

  • Export (Me → Privacy & data → Export): Download a file containing all your data: birth details and birth-time inference records are decrypted; end-to-end encrypted content is exported as encrypted data your devices can open; and your files, including photos, are included as download links.

12. Age

  • Minimum age 13 for an account: Having an account requires you to be at least 13 years old, determined from the birth date on your own profile.
  • Under 13: If your profile shows an age under 13, Armilla works only on your device: no sign-in, sync, AI features, invitations, purchases, or palm & face features, and no usage statistics. An existing account is paused: you can still export your data, delete your account, or correct the birth date, and we do not delete it automatically.
  • Ages 13–17: Accounts aged 13–17 have no AI features at all (Ask Armilla, AI readings and analyses, AI palm & face reading, or birth-time inference), cannot invite others to fill in their details, and have no Pro workspace. Usage data is off by default unless they turn it on.
  • Invitations and minors' profiles: Invited people must confirm they are 18 or older. Profiles of minors never reach any AI feature, and share images (essence card, six-rings card) are never made for a minor's profile.

13. This website, the web app, and invitation pages

  • Pages at arcturatech.com/armilla (hosted on GitHub Pages): These pages use no cookies, ads, or analytics and load no third-party scripts. The invitation and compatibility result pages talk only to our server: the invitation token sits after the "#" in the link, so it is not sent to the host or written to its logs, and the page removes it from the address bar once read. The result token is kept in your browser's local storage until it expires (30 days), and your language choice is kept there too.
