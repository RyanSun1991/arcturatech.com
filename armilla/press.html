璇玑 Armilla 1.0 — 媒体资料页

以单一确定性引擎精算命盘，汇聚东西方十套体系与句句溯源的克制 AI 解读。

产品简介与适用人群

  • 十套体系： 「璇玑」源自古代观天仪器（《尚书·舜典》「在璇玑玉衡，以齐七政」），与西方浑天仪之名 Armilla 呼应。璇玑 Armilla 在 iPhone、Android 与网页端计算六套命盘（八字／四柱、紫微斗数、西洋占星、吠陀占星、人类图、数字命理），并行易经、心理测评（16型人格、九型人格、大五人格、依恋类型、价值观、DISC）及手相与面相（形相，仅限账号本人，单独同意后由视觉模型读取）。
  • 空间与人群： 命盘（离线排盘与时间层）、解读（跨体系或古法解读）、命途（过往与未来十年时间轴对照事件）、关系（六维合盘与 3–8 人群盘，永不打总分）、今日 / 觉察（每日指引与约 30 秒日志）与问璇玑（随页问答并附出处）面向研习者、自我探索者、转折期人群、伴侣／家庭／合伙人、日常觉察用户及专业命理师／教练（Pro 网页工作台）。

核心差异与技术标准

  • 单一 Rust 引擎： 纯 Rust 天文算法（VSOP87、ELP2000-82B/MPP02、Meeus）、IANA 历史时区与夏令时、真太阳时、精确至分节气及含闰月农历；离线运行于手机（UniFFI）及 WebAssembly，对照 1,079 组参考出生时刻零误差。
  • AI 绝不自行推算： AI 功能采用 OpenAI 或 Google Gemini，由璇玑按功能设定（用户不可自选，提供方不将接收内容用于训练）：文字功能（解读、问璇玑、今日指引，以及出生时间推定的提问与理由）向提供方发送引擎算出的命盘数据与问题（绝不发送姓名、出生日期、出生时间与出生地点），日志与人生事件文本仅在开启「让 AI 读取我的人生事件」后发送；形相仅在另行同意后发送手相或面相照片。每句命盘陈述附可点击出处，无依据句子展示前自动剔除。
  • 实证对照与时间推定： 「汇聚」圆点标示多体系共识；记录事件可统计体系契合度。出生时间推定结合家人记忆、5 件以上事件、区分性问答与可选形相参考缩小范围（AI 辅助或纯本地无 AI；依模拟回测分级，不宣称命中率）。
  • 隐私与明确边界： 排盘、导出与日志无需注册；支持无密码登录；同步日志、事件笔记与照片端到端加密，可随时导出或删除。严守六大不预测： 死亡与寿限、疾病诊断、灾祸意外、官司结果、具体投资标的、彩票赌博（形相亦不判健康与寿命）。无周订阅、不售开运物、无广告。

Armilla 1.0 — Press Fact Sheet

Ten traditions, one deterministic chart engine, and cited AI readings tracing every chart claim to its source.

What It Is & Who It Is For

  • Ten systems: Named for armilla and xuán jī (璇玑), Armilla computes six charts—BaZi (Four Pillars), Zi Wei Dou Shu, Western & Vedic astrology, Energy Blueprint, numerology—plus the I Ching, psychometrics (16 Types, Enneagram, Big Five, attachment style, values, DISC), and Hand & face (vision model, opt-in, account holder only).
  • Spaces & users: Charts (offline cycles), Readings, Life Map (past & 10-year timelines), People (6-axis pair & 3–8 group charts, never one score), Today (prompt, ~30s journal), Ask (cited Q&A)—for students, self-explorers, people at turning points, couples, families, founders, and pros (Pro web workspace).

What Sets It Apart

  • Verified Rust engine: Pure Rust astronomy (VSOP87, ELP2000-82B/MPP02, Meeus), IANA time zones/DST, true solar time, minute-accurate solar terms, and lunar leap months—offline on phones (UniFFI) and WebAssembly; zero differences across 1,079 reference birth moments.
  • Cited AI, zero math: Armilla sets per feature whether OpenAI or Google's Gemini serves it (users do not choose; neither trains on what it receives): for text features (readings, Ask, today's guidance, and birth-time inference questions and reasons), the provider receives computed chart data and the question (never names, birth date, birth time, or birthplace), plus journal and life-event text only when "Let AI read my life events" is turned on; for Hand & face, it receives palm or face photos only after separate consent. Every chart claim cites a tappable element; unsourced lines are stripped.
  • Lived proof: Convergence dots mark cross-system agreement; logged events track accuracy. Birth-time inference narrows unknown hours via family clues, 5+ events, distinguishing questions, and optional Hand & face reference (AI-assisted or local; tiered confidence from simulated backtest, no hit rate claimed).
  • Privacy & limits: No account for charts/export/journal; passwordless login; synced journal entries, life-event notes, and life-event photos use E2E encryption; palm and face photos for Hand & face are uploaded after separate consent to the user's private cloud storage so the AI provider's vision model can read them (not E2E encrypted, deletable at any time); in-app export/delete. Never predicts: death/lifespan, illness/diagnosis, disasters/accidents, lawsuit outcomes, specific investments, or lotteries/gambling (nor health/lifespan in Hand & face); no ritual goods or ads.

  • Plans (USD; local currency; 7-day yearly trial; no weekly): Free ($0): offline charts, export, 6 Brief/day, Ask (3/day), decade Life Map, 3 profiles, journal. Plus ($9.99/mo · $59.99/yr): Full/custom & 4 Long/mo, full Life Map, unlimited Ask, full pair, 30 profiles, 1 annual report & inference/yr. Family ($16.99/mo · $99.99/yr): Plus for 6 accounts, 3–8 charts, family space, 60 profiles, 3 inferences/yr. Pro ($29.99/mo · $239/yr): Web workspace (1,000 clients, 12-person, white-label PDF, JSON; no API), 30 Long/mo, unlimited reports/inferences (fair use).
  • One-time (no subscription): In-depth Essence Report ($29), In-depth Pair Report ($19), Family / Team Report ($39), Parent & Child Report ($29), Year-Ahead Report ($19), Business Partner Report ($29), Long Reading ($4.99), Birth-time Inference ($19, 30-day session).
