HELP & SUPPORT

帮助与支持

先看看下面的常见问题；没找到答案就写信给我们：support@arcturatech.com。我们通常在 2 个工作日内回复。

写信时请附上：设备型号、系统版本、璇玑的版本（我 → 关于），以及问题出现的步骤或截图。请不要发送恢复短语——我们永远不会向你索要它；也请不要在邮件里附上他人的出生资料。

开始使用

一定要注册账号吗？

不用。不登录也能完整排盘、写日志、记录人生事迹，数据只存在这台设备上。想在多台设备间同步、使用「问璇玑」、邀请亲友或购买会员时再登录（Apple、Google 或邮箱验证码）。

不知道准确的出生时间怎么办？

可以选择「±15 分钟」「±1 小时」「只知时辰」「不知道」或「只知年份」。受时间影响的结果会标出来或以虚线显示；累计记录五件人生事迹后，还可以用「出生时间校正」帮你缩小范围。

排盘准确吗？

所有盘面都由同一个排盘引擎在设备上计算，处理真太阳时、历史时区与夏令时、精确到分钟的节气和农历（含闰月）。不同流派的参数（宫位制、岁差、子时等）可以在设置里切换。

为什么不同体系说的不一样？

每个体系从不同角度描述一个人，本来就会有差异。璇玑把几个体系同时指向的地方标为「共振」，并附上依据；它描述的是倾向，不是判语。

邀请与共享

对方收到邀请后要做什么？

打开链接即可，不用注册：选择共享级别、填写出生信息、勾选同意即可完成。已安装璇玑时会直接在 App 里打开。完成后对方会得到一个 30 天内有效的结果链接。

怎样停止共享？

邀请人在对方的档案页选择「停止使用 TA 的资料」；被邀请人可以用原邀请链接撤回，或在「关系」里停止共享。撤回后对方的出生资料与相关结果会在 24 小时内删除。

为什么我不能邀请别人或使用「问璇玑」？

未满 18 岁的账户不开放 AI 对话、手相面相、邀请他人填写与专业版工作台；完全本地模式下，所有联网功能都会暂停。

会员与购买

怎样取消订阅？

在 App Store 购买：iPhone 设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅。在 Google Play 购买：Google Play →「付款和订阅」。在网页版购买：网页版账户页一键取消。也可以在 App 的 我 → 会员 里找到对应入口。删除账号不会自动取消商店订阅。

换了手机，会员不见了？

请用同一个账号登录，然后在 我 → 会员 点「恢复购买」。家庭成员的会员由家庭主账号管理。

免费试用什么时候扣费？

年付方案的 7 天试用结束时开始按年扣费；结束前 2 天我们会提醒你。不想继续的话，在试用结束前取消即可，不会产生费用。

可以退款吗？

App Store 与 Google Play 的退款由 Apple 和 Google 依其政策处理，请向商店申请。网页版购买的问题请写信给我们。

同步、加密与数据

恢复短语是什么？丢了怎么办？

日志、事迹笔记等内容是端到端加密的，恢复短语是在没有旧设备时解开它们的唯一方式。只要还有一台已登录的设备，就可以在 我 → 安全 里设置新的恢复短语。所有设备都丢失且没有恢复短语时，加密内容无法找回，我们也无能为力。

怎样导出或删除我的数据？

我 → 隐私 → 导出我的数据 / 删除账号。详见删除账号与数据和隐私政策。

Help & Support

Start with the questions below. If you don’t find your answer, write to support@arcturatech.com — we usually reply within 2 business days.

Please include your device model, system version, Armilla version (Me › About) and the steps or a screenshot of the problem. Never send your recovery phrase — we will never ask for it — and please don’t include other people’s birth details in your email.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. You can chart, journal and log life events without signing in; your data then stays on this device. Sign in (Apple, Google or an email code) when you want to sync between devices, use Ask Armilla, invite someone or buy a membership.

I don’t know my exact birth time.

Choose “±15 minutes”, “±1 hour”, “double-hour only”, “unknown” or “year only”. Results that depend on the time are marked or drawn dashed, and after five life events, birth-time rectification can help narrow it down.

How accurate are the charts?

Every chart is computed on your device by one calculation engine that handles true solar time, historical time zones and daylight saving, solar terms to the minute and the lunar calendar with leap months. School-specific settings (house system, ayanamsa, the Zi hour and so on) can be changed in Settings.

Why do the systems say different things?

Each system describes a person from its own angle, so they differ. Armilla marks the places where several systems point the same way as “resonance” and shows the evidence. It describes tendencies, not verdicts.

Invitations and sharing

What does the person I invite need to do?

Just open the link — no account needed. They choose what to share, enter their birth details, agree, and they’re done. If they have Armilla installed, the link opens in the app. Afterwards they get a result link that works for 30 days.

How do I stop sharing?

As the inviter, choose “Stop using their details” on their profile. As the invitee, use your original invitation link or stop sharing under People. Their birth details and the related results are then deleted within 24 hours.

Why can’t I invite people or use Ask Armilla?

Accounts under 18 can’t use AI conversations, palm and face reading, invitations for others to fill in, or the Pro workspace. In fully local mode, every online feature is paused.

Memberships and purchases

How do I cancel a subscription?

Bought on the App Store: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Bought on Google Play: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Bought on the web: one click on your account page on the web. Me › Membership in the app links to the right place. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a store subscription.

My membership is missing on a new phone.

Sign in with the same account, then tap “Restore purchases” in Me › Membership. Family members’ memberships are managed by the family’s plan holder.

When does the free trial start charging?

The yearly plan’s 7-day trial converts to a yearly charge when it ends, and we remind you 2 days before. Cancel before it ends and you won’t be charged.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle App Store and Google Play refunds under their policies — please ask the store. For web purchases, write to us.

Sync, encryption and your data

What is the recovery phrase, and what if I lose it?

Your journal, event notes and other content are end-to-end encrypted; the recovery phrase is the only way to unlock them without an existing device. As long as one signed-in device remains, you can set a new phrase in Me › Security. If every device is lost and there is no recovery phrase, the encrypted content can’t be recovered — not even by us.

How do I export or delete my data?

Me › Privacy › Export my data / Delete account. See Delete your account and data and the Privacy Policy.
