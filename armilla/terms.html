1. What Armilla is and isn't

Armilla computes charts across multiple systems using one calculation engine—Western and Vedic astrology, BaZi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Energy Blueprint, numerology, the I Ching, Palm & face, and psychometric questionnaires—and provides a space to record life events and daily reflections, explore relationship charts (including People and Family space), and use Ask Armilla across Today, Map, People, Path, and Life Map. It describes tendencies, cycles, and the leaning of a period for self-understanding and reflection. Every chart value comes from our calculation engine, and every chart statement the AI makes points to an element the engine computed. When Armilla describes "resonance," it means several systems point the same way—it is not a verdict on a life.

Ask Armilla answers questions on any subject, including questions about the future, by offering a direction and a leaning grounded in the chart. However, Armilla strictly does not do any of the following:

  • No prohibited predictions: Armilla never predicts death, illness, disaster, the outcome of a lawsuit, which investment to pick, or lottery results.
  • No relationship score: Armilla gives no single score for a relationship.
  • No health or lifespan judgements in palm and face readings: Palm & face and AI palm & face reading never judge health or how long someone will live.
  • Not professional advice: Armilla is not medical, psychological, legal, financial, or other professional advice, does not diagnose any condition, and does not replace a qualified professional. Anyone in danger should contact local emergency services immediately.
  • No decisions about others' life opportunities: Armilla must not be used to decide anything about other people's employment, housing, credit, insurance, or similar matters.
