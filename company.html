Company

Software you can navigate by.

Arctura Technologies Inc. is an AI-native Canadian software corporation headquartered in Ottawa. We exist to make personal health legible with intelligence people can trust — and to prove that AI products can be both world-class and worthy of that trust.

Our story

Named for the northern star.

Arctura joins arc — the geometry every screen we draw is built from — with Arcturus, the brightest star of the northern sky, used by navigators for millennia. It's a promise about the kind of software we make: precise in construction, steady in direction.

We started with a conviction: the most personal data people have — their health story — deserves software with the polish of the world's best consumer products and the restraint of a fiduciary. Too much health software is either clinical and unusable, or engaging and extractive. We build the third thing.

Today that conviction ships as Linnea, our health record & score platform, distributed worldwide through the App Store — with AI woven through the roadmap and more products in the studio behind it.

Values

What we optimize for.

Trust before growth

We'd rather earn a smaller number of people's complete confidence than a large number's passing attention. No dark patterns, no data resale, ever.

Owner's discipline

We operate what we build. Every architectural decision is made by people who will be on call for it and paying its cloud bill years from now.
