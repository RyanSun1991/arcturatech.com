Solutions

Six practices. One integrated software house.

Most companies split strategy, design, engineering, data, and operations across departments and vendors. We run them as one discipline — so decisions stay coherent from the first sketch to the millionth session.

01

Product Engineering

Native mobile · App Store commerce · release engineering

We build native software the platform-owner way: Swift and SwiftUI on iOS, engineered for launch-day stability and year-five maintainability. Subscription commerce, entitlements, paywalls, and App Store operations are core competencies, not afterthoughts.

Native iOS applications (Swift / SwiftUI) Subscription & in-app purchase infrastructure App Store submission, review & lifecycle ops Automated CI/CD & phased release management Performance profiling & crash-free targets Long-term codebase stewardship
02

Health Platforms

Personal records · device ecosystems · longitudinal insight

Health data is fragmented across clinics, wearables, and memory. We engineer systems that unify it into a single, private, patient-owned record — then make it legible through scores, trends, and timely nudges that respect clinical boundaries.

Personal health record (PHR) architecture HealthKit, sensor & wearable integration Longitudinal trend & baseline modelling Medication, measurement & visit tracking Intelligent reminders without notification fatigue Clear non-diagnostic information design
03

Data & Intelligence

Scoring systems · applied ML · analytics

A number people trust is harder to build than a model that merely predicts. Our intelligence work turns heterogeneous signals into composite scores and insights that are explainable, stable, and honest about uncertainty — with privacy-preserving computation as the default.

Composite scoring engine design On-device & privacy-safe machine learning Product analytics & experimentation pipelines Anomaly & trend detection Explainability & score-integrity reviews Data contracts & quality monitoring
04

Cloud & Infrastructure

Sync architecture · security · operations

Consumer software earns trust by simply working — on a subway, on a new phone, three years later. We run globally distributed, security-hardened backends with realtime sync, disciplined observability, and the cost discipline of a company that pays its own cloud bill.

Realtime sync & offline-first architecture Postgres data platforms & edge functions Authentication & identity infrastructure Encryption in transit & at rest Observability, SLOs & incident response Backup, retention & disaster recovery

Experience Design

Design systems & token architecture Interaction & motion design Data visualization for non-experts Accessibility (WCAG) by default Brand identity & wordmark systems Design–engineering integration
