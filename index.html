Principles

How we build.

Four commitments that shape every product decision we make.

Privacy by default

Personal data is a responsibility, not an asset. We collect the minimum, encrypt what we keep, and never sell it.

Craft over noise

Fewer features, finished properly. We sweat typography, motion, and the empty states nobody screenshots.

Long-term software

Products are commitments. We maintain what we ship, version thoughtfully, and don't chase trends off cliffs.

Clarity wins

If a screen needs a manual, it isn't done. We design so the next step is obvious and the data speaks plainly.

Company

A small studio with a north star.

Arctura Technologies Inc. is an independent software company incorporated in Ontario, Canada. The name joins arc — the geometry we build with — and Arcturus, the northern star long used to navigate by.

We design, engineer, and publish our own consumer products. Small on purpose: every product gets the founder's full attention, and quality is never someone else's department.

We're based in Ottawa and ship worldwide through the App Store.
