Linnea

HEALTH RECORD & SCORE

Your health story, in one private place. Linnea unifies measurements, records, and daily signals into a clear timeline — and distills everything into a single score that tells you where you stand and where you're heading.

  • Unified record: measurements, notes, visits, meds
  • Health Score with transparent contributing factors
  • Trends & baselines across weeks and years
  • Reminders that respect your attention
  • Secure cloud sync across your devices
  • Private by design — never sold, never advertised on
