1. What the app is

The app is a self-awareness and practice tool. It helps you pause when emotions run high, look at what's going on, and note a line afterwards, and it offers breathing, attention and other self-help practices, reviews and a private journal.

The app is not a medical device. It doesn't provide medical or psychological diagnosis or treatment, and it isn't a substitute for professional help (see section 8). If you're in real distress or thinking about harming yourself or others, contact local emergency services or the support lines on the app's crisis safety card right away (see section 9).
