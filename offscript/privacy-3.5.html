Privacy Policy

Offscript · Arctura Technologies Inc. · Effective October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Offscript app on iOS, iPadOS, Apple Watch and Android, and to related websites and services (together, the "Services"). The Services are provided by Arctura Technologies Inc. ("Arctura," "we," "us" or "our").

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information. You can use Offscript without an account. If you sign in with your email address, we collect that address so we can send you a sign-in code. If you use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, we receive an account identifier from Apple or Google and, from Apple, any email address Apple shares with us. Each account also has a randomly generated account number.
  • Content you sync or back up. If you sign in, your records and what the app organizes from them (such as titles, feelings, themes and settings) are encrypted on your device before they're synced to your other devices. The same applies to the optional encrypted backup where the app offers it. We store only the encrypted copy and can't read its contents. Some of this content, such as the moods and feelings you note, may be considered health information.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Offscript Plus, Apple processes the payment and gives us information about the transaction, such as the product, dates and subscription status. We don't receive your payment card details. One-time purchases are verified with Apple on your device and aren't sent to us.
  • Messages to us. If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical information. When the app connects to our servers, for example to sign in or sync, we receive information such as your IP address, device type and the time of the request. We use it to keep you signed in, operate the Services and prevent abuse.
  • Sync information. To sync, we keep a random identifier for each device you sign in on, along with limited information about your encrypted records, such as how many there are, their approximate size and when they changed, but not what they say.
  • AI usage records. If you use AI features, we keep a record of each use, such as when it ran, what kind of run it was and how much processing it used. These records don't contain anything you wrote. We use them to apply usage limits and manage costs.
  • Weekly anonymous counts. Once a week, the app may send us a few anonymous counts about how its features are used, for example how many days you opened the app that week. These counts are numbers only: they contain no account, no device identifier and none of your content, and we don't link them to you. You can turn them off at any time in the app's privacy settings.
  • Websites. When you visit our websites, our hosting and font providers may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the page.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and we do not use your device's advertising identifier.

Information from third parties

We receive information from Apple or Google when you use them to sign in, and from Apple about your subscription (such as renewals, expirations and refunds), as described above.

Information that stays on your device

Offscript processes your content on your device. Transcribing what you say, sorting and organizing your records, search, and writing moods to Apple Health all happen on your device. Offscript only writes to Apple Health and never reads from it. Voice recordings and photos are never uploaded to our servers. Your other content leaves your device only as described in this policy: encrypted, if you sign in to sync or turn on backup, or as de-identified summaries, if you turn on AI features.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide, maintain and operate the Services, including sign-in, sync, purchases and AI features;
  • verify subscriptions and apply usage limits;
  • send sign-in codes and respond to your messages;
  • keep the Services secure and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • understand, in aggregate, how features are used, so we can improve the Services;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or use it to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. We don't use your content to train AI models.

3. AI features

Offscript Plus includes optional AI features, called deep insights in the cloud, that look for themes and changes in your records over time. They are off by default.

Only after you turn on AI features and agree on the in-app consent screen does the app prepare short summaries of your records on your device, for example a short title, the type of record, the feeling and the date. Before anything is sent, the app replaces people's names with placeholders and removes contact details on your device. The consent screen shows what is sent. The app sends these summaries through our servers to our AI provider, OpenAI, which generates the insights and returns them to your device. Your own words, voice recordings and photos are never sent.

  • We don't keep the summaries or the results on our servers. Results are stored on your device and are encrypted if they sync.
  • OpenAI processes the summaries on our behalf and doesn't use them to train its models. To monitor for abuse, OpenAI may keep them for up to 30 days before deleting them. Each request includes a pseudonymous identifier that OpenAI uses to prevent abuse; it contains nothing you wrote.
  • Automatic de-identification may not catch every name or detail.
  • Because summaries can include feelings, which may be sensitive, we rely on your consent for this feature. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning AI features off in the app. This stops new summaries from being sent and doesn't affect processing that took place before.

AI features may not be available in every country or region.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only as described below.

Service providers

We use service providers to run the Services. They process information on our behalf and only on our instructions, and they are bound by contract to protect it in a manner consistent with this policy.

  • Supabase hosts our servers and database for sign-in, sync and subscription checks, in the United States.
  • OpenAI processes summaries for AI features, only if you turn them on.
  • Google sends our sign-in code emails.

Apple and Google

Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, In-App Purchase, iCloud Keychain (where the key that encrypts your synced content is stored), Apple Health and other system features you choose to use are provided by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Other sharing

  • At your direction. When you choose to share something, such as a card or an export, it goes where you send it.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if we believe it's required by law or legal process, or necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, us or others.
  • Business transfers. If we're involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Your choices and rights

In the app, you can use Offscript without an account, sign out, turn AI features on or off, turn off the weekly anonymous counts, export your records, delete records or your account, and manage permissions such as microphone, camera, Health and notifications in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw your consent. You can do much of this directly in the app. For anything else, email privacy@arcturatech.com. We may need to verify your request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority; in Canada, that's the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

If you're in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process personal information to perform our contract with you (for your account, sync and purchases), with your consent (for AI features), and for our legitimate interests (such as security and understanding aggregate use). California residents have the right to know, correct and delete their personal information and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined under California law.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Content on your device stays there until you delete it or delete the app.
  • We keep personal information on our servers for as long as your account exists or as needed to provide the Services, and longer only where needed to comply with the law, resolve disputes or enforce our agreements. Encrypted backups are kept until you delete them in the app.
  • When you delete synced records in the app, they're deleted from our servers too.
  • Deleting your account. You can delete your account in the app at any time, or ask us by email. Deleting your account deletes the data on our servers associated with it, including your encrypted records, device list, purchase records and AI usage records, except where we're required by law to keep it. Copies may remain in logs or backups for a limited period. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an Offscript Plus subscription; you manage that through Apple.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period. Because the weekly anonymous counts aren't linked to you, we can't identify or delete yours; they're kept for a limited period and then deleted.
  • Withdrawing consent. You can turn off AI features or the weekly anonymous counts in the app at any time.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. Content you sync or back up is encrypted on your device before it's uploaded, and we don't have the key. Data is also encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Please keep your devices and the accounts you use to sign in secure.

8. International transfers

We are based in Canada. Our servers are in the United States, and our service providers may process information in other countries; for example, OpenAI processes requests in its facilities around the world. Where required by law, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as contractual protections with our service providers.

9. Children

The Services aren't directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age that applies where you live, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at privacy@arcturatech.com and we'll take steps to delete it.
