Delete your account

Offscript · Arctura Technologies Inc.

You can use Offscript without an account. If you signed in, you can delete your account at any time.

In the app

On iPhone, iPad or Android, open Offscript and go to Me › Account and devices › Delete account. Read what will be deleted, then confirm.

By email

If you can't open the app, email privacy@arcturatech.com with the subject "Delete my Offscript account". Write from the email address you signed in with, or tell us whether you used Apple, Google or email to sign in. We may need to confirm the request is yours before we delete anything.

What gets deleted

  • The data on our servers linked to your account: your encrypted records, your device list, purchase records and AI usage records, except where the law requires us to keep something.
  • Copies may stay in logs or backups for a limited time before they're removed.
  • Records on your devices stay there until you delete them or delete the app.

Before you delete

  • Deleting your account doesn't cancel Offscript Plus. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
  • Want to keep your records? Export them in the app first.
  • To delete only some records, delete them in the app. Synced records you delete are removed from our servers too.

删除照见账号

照见不登录也能用。登录过的话，随时可以删除账号。

在 App 里：打开照见，进入我 › 账号与设备 › 删除账号，看过会删掉什么，再确认。

发邮件：打不开 App 的话，用登录时的邮箱写信到 privacy@arcturatech.com，标题写「删除照见账号」；或者告诉我们你是用 Apple、Google 还是邮箱登录的。删除之前我们可能要先确认是你本人。

会删掉：服务器上和这个账号有关的数据——加密的记录、设备列表、购买记录、AI 用量记录，法律要求保留的除外。日志和备份里的副本会在一段时间后清掉。手机上的记录不受影响，你删掉或卸载 App 才会没有。

删之前：删除账号不会取消照见 Plus，先去 App Store 或 Google Play 的订阅里取消。想留着记录，先在 App 里导出。只想删几条，在 App 里删掉就行，同步过的也会从服务器上删掉。
