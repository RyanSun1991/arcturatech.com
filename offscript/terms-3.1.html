1. What the app is

The app is a self-awareness and practice tool. It helps you note things as they come up, pause when emotions run high, look at what's going on, and note a line afterwards, and it offers breathing, attention and other self-help practices, reviews and a private journal. Your records are sorted on your device, and things that keep coming up are put side by side for you (see section 8). Once you sign in, your records are encrypted on your device and then synced between the devices you sign in on with the same account (see section 4).

The app is not a medical device. It doesn't provide medical or psychological diagnosis or treatment, and it isn't a substitute for professional help (see section 9). If you're in real distress or thinking about harming yourself or others, contact local emergency services or the support lines on the app's crisis safety card right away (see section 10).

You must meet the age rating shown on the App Store product page (13 in most regions), or the higher age at which you may consent to an online service where you live.

3. Your account

  • From version 3.1, you need to sign in to use the app. The account is used to encrypt and sync your records to your own devices (see section 4) and to manage the devices you're signed in on.
  • Before you sign in, the first screen still lets you Pause, open Rescue, and open the crisis safety card ("Talk to someone"). None of these needs an account or a connection.

When you sign in with Apple or Google, that step is provided by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. What we keep for your account is set out in section 4 of the Privacy Policy.

4. Sync and your key

  • Once you sign in, your records and what the app sorts out from them are encrypted on your device and synced to the other devices you sign in on with the same account. Voice recordings, relationship mirror entries, the search index and a few other items stay on the device and never sync, and you can keep any dream out of sync. Sections 3 and 5 of the Privacy Policy list what syncs and what doesn't.
  • The key used for this encryption lives in your iCloud Keychain. We never receive it, and we can't read your content. If you lose your iCloud Keychain and have no other signed-in device, the copy on our server can't be opened again, and we have no way to recover it for you. The records on your devices aren't affected.
  • Sync needs a connection and may be delayed or interrupted. If two devices change the same record at the same time, the most recent change wins and the other version stays on that device. We work to keep sync available but can't promise it will never be interrupted; you can export the records you care about at any time (see section 6).
  • Each account has a storage limit on our server. When it's full, new records are kept only on your device for now. A single record too long to sync stays only on your device.
  • When you sign out, the records on that device stay and the app returns to the sign-in screen. On the Account page you can remove another device or sign out all your other devices; records on those devices aren't deleted.

5. Deleting your account

  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Me › Account › Delete account. Your account, all of your encrypted records, keys and device list on our server, and the details we keep for your account are erased right away and can't be recovered, along with any encrypted backup from version 1.0 on our server that this device can identify. The key in your iCloud Keychain is deleted too, and iCloud Keychain removes it from all your Apple devices.

8. Sorting on your device, and reading dreams

  • On your device, the app sorts out a title, type, feeling, and the people and topics for each record, and may add a possible reading and a practice you could try. From your records it also puts together today's observation, the evening review, a weekly letter, "You said", themes, and how the month went. Which records, how many times and when are worked out by fixed rules. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the app uses Apple's on-device model to title and sort records and to smooth the wording of prepared sentences. On devices without Apple Intelligence, it sorts by keywords and the tags you pick. The sorting happens on your device; what it produces is encrypted before it syncs, just like your records, so we can't read it.
  • This sorting, and the readings, observations and suggestions that come with it, may be inaccurate. A type may be wrong, a title may miss the point, an observation may leave out what matters to you, and a reading or suggestion may not fit you. They're there for you to consider, not a judgment or conclusion about you.
  • You can change them: titles, types, feelings, people and topics, and theme names. Your changes take priority, and later sorting won't overwrite them. You can delete them: a reading, an observation, or everything of one kind when you turn it off in Me › AI and privacy. You can turn them off: each item in Me › AI and privacy can be turned off on its own, and anything you mark "Don't show this" stops appearing until you bring it back.
  • On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Dreams › Read a dream uses Apple's on-device model to suggest a few questions and some possible readings. The questions and readings are only ideas to consider, not interpretations, predictions or diagnoses. They may be inaccurate or not fit you, and what your dream means is up to you. On devices without Apple Intelligence, only the questions are shown.

10. Crisis resources

The check can miss things and can misread them. It isn't a monitoring or alert service, and we won't contact you, your contacts or anyone else because of it.
  • The support lines on the safety card are run by third parties. We can't guarantee their availability, waiting times or response. In an emergency, call your local emergency number directly.
